How this was mapped
  • Walked and photographed on 21 November 2026. Photos along the right-of-way are placed by their timestamps on the walk, since the phone's GPS scattered shots up to 70 ft. Photos off the walk keep their GPS fix, except the ones at the west end of the boat row: those are set on the boatyard lot line where they show the photographer standing, on the yard edge or just inside the knotweed.
  • Each photo is set on the 2022 lidar point cloud at its position and compass heading, and what it shows (species, how dense) is mapped onto the vegetation it actually sees.
  • Severity: 60% how dense the invasives are in the photos, 25% how thick the lidar eye-level layer is, 15% how many target species share the spot. Common reed and knotweed are cut out of the layer, except the honeysuckle and rose patches the photos pick out inside them.
  • Lot lines are the Town of Tiverton's Plat 103 (8 Apr 2026), registered to RIGIS parcels; they are assessor lines, not a survey.
  • Withheld: photo locations, photos with people, houses or signs, and stone features. Cultural information stays under PPLT and Tribal governance.
